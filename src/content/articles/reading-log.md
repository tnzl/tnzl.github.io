A personal reading log does not need to be a literature review. It needs enough signal that future-you remembers *why* a paper mattered.

## Format that works

- **One-line claim**
- **What I would steal** (idea, trick, measurement)
- **Open question**

Examples of themes that tend to stick in compiler/inference work: attention approximations, quantization numerics, memory-bound kernel design, and IR design for extensibility.
