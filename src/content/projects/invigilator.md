Invigilator monitors student activity in real time during online exams and generates warnings when unfair means are detected.

## Highlights

- Frame-in / warnings-out API (`give_image`)
- Face, gaze, and anti-spoofing oriented signals
- Designed for interactive use on ordinary CPUs
- Privacy-minded design: no persistent image/video storage in the demo flow

Useful as a compact computer-vision systems project from applied internship work.
